Lumen Vault

Laser escape puzzle · Free

Mira's first night shift at the city museum goes wrong when an old lantern wakes up and the security lasers seal every door. Bend the light with mirrors, dodge the creatures that patrol each room, and find your way out.

Every room is solvable
Each of the 160 rooms is checked by a solver, and they get harder as you go.
Think before you move
Creatures step when you step. Limited sparks and five hearts make every move count.
New tricks each world
Crumbling floors, prisms, ice, clockwork mirrors and pressure-plate gates.
Secrets to find
Five hidden easter eggs unlock secret lantern colours.
A new room every day
The Daily Vault gives everyone the same fresh puzzle each day.

Privacy policy for Lumen Vault

Last updated: November 8, 2026

No account neededProgress stays on your deviceAds by Google AdMobAnonymous gameplay stats

Lumen Vault (“the game”) is made by Mirrorlight Studio (“we”, “us”) for Android, iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (rooms completed, stars, hearts, lantern colours, secrets found and settings) is saved on your device. A backup copy is kept in the app's own storage on the same device, so progress isn't lost if the phone clears space. This progress stays on your device: we do not run our own servers, and the game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or precise location. The services listed below (Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and RevenueCat) run on their own servers.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads and the app. Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Gameplay statistics

From version 1.3, the game sends anonymous gameplay events, such as when a room is started, won or failed, to Google Analytics for Firebase. This shows us which rooms are too hard so we can improve them. Firebase may also receive basic device information such as device model, operating system version, approximate region and an app instance ID. Collection of the advertising ID by Firebase and ad personalisation signals are turned off, and these statistics are not linked to your name.

We also use Firebase Remote Config to adjust game settings, such as how often ads appear, without an app update. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and by the App Store on iPhone and iPad. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, but not your payment details. Learn more at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • On iPhone and iPad, you can change tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Where available, you can change your ad consent choices from the privacy choices button in the game's settings.
  • You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage.
  • You can contact us at any time with questions or requests about your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
