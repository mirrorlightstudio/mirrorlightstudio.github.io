Privacy policy for Lumen Vault

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Lumen Vault (“the game”) is made by Mirrorlight Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (rooms completed, stars, hearts, lantern colours, secrets found and settings) is saved on your device. In the Android app, a backup copy is kept in the app's own storage on the same device. We do not run servers that collect this information, and the game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or location.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads and the app. Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, but not your payment details. You can learn more at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage.
  • You can contact us at any time with questions or requests about your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
