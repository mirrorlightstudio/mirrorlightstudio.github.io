Knuckle Highway

Race. Punch. Survive.

Knuckle Highway is a motorcycle racing game where the race is a fight. Line up with a pack of rival riders, open the throttle and knock them off their bikes before they do it to you. Grab a weapon from an armed rival, outrun the police, and earn coins for faster bikes and fresh paint.

Fight while you ride
Punch, kick and elbow rivals at full speed. Steal a club, chain, pipe or crowbar from an armed rider.
8 roads to master
City streets, mountain passes, a canyon tunnel, the countryside and neon nights, each with its own traffic.
Build your garage
Win coins to unlock new bikes, upgrade them and paint them your way.
Day, night, rain and snow
Every road changes with the time of day and the weather.
World Series
Race every road back to back and finish on top. Daily rewards and challenges keep it fresh.
Race your friends
Share a room code to race friends online, or play on the same Wi-Fi.
