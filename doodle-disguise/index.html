Doodle Disguise

Paint yourself. Hide in plain sight.

In Doodle Disguise, the hiders get a few moments to paint their own disguise, then try to blend into a busy scene before the seekers start looking. Paint yourself like a speaker stack, a lantern or a coffee cup, stand very still and hope nobody looks twice. The disguises are half the fun: some are genius, most are hilarious.

Paint your own disguise
Lots of brushes, colours, patterns and stickers. The more you try, the funnier it gets.
Hide or seek
Blend into the scene as a hider, or hunt down the doodles as a seeker.
5 scenes at launch
Music Festival, Coffee Rave, House Party, Diwali Market and Disco Night.
You set the paint time
Quick 30-second scribbles or careful 3-minute masterpieces.
Play with anyone
Play with your friends, or join players online from around the world.
Phone, laptop or TV
Made to fit every screen, so the whole room can play together.
